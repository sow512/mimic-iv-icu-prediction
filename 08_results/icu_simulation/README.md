# ICU Capacity Simulation Results

This folder contains results from hypothetical ICU resource-planning simulations integrating predicted ICU-transfer risk with bed availability and capacity constraints.

## Analyses

- ICU demand surges from 1× to 5×.
- Bed occupancy and availability under different capacity scenarios.
- Immediate admissions, delayed admissions and unmet ICU requests.
- Risk-based patient prioritization versus random allocation.
- Risk-weighted unmet demand and ICU shortage probabilities.

## Key Findings

Increasing ICU demand led to greater bed shortages, particularly when initial occupancy was high.

In the illustrated simulations, risk-based prioritization left less predicted ICU-transfer risk unserved than random ordering.

## Limitations

These simulations are hypothetical. Risk-based allocation is based on predicted ICU-transfer probability, not clinical urgency. Results do not establish improved clinical outcomes, and the random-order comparison was not performed using fully matched simulation scenarios.

No restricted MIMIC-IV patient-level data are included.
